
## **CIA** #cia
1. (C) Which stands for **Confidentiality** : Ensuring that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized individuals or systems.
2. (I) Which stands for **Integrity** : Ensuring data is accurate, consistent, and unaltered unless authorized.
3. (A) Which stands for **Availability** : Ensuring systems and data are accessible when needed by authorized users.

## **GRC** #grc
#### Stands for : **Governance**, **Risk Management**, and **Compliance**.
1. **Governance** :
   - Involves establishing and enforcing policies and procedures to ensure proper management of resources and operations.
   - Focuses on ensuring that the organization follows best practices in decision-making and business management.
2. **Risk Management** : 
   - The process of identifying and assessing potential risks that could affect the organization, such as financial, technical, or legal risks.
   - Includes strategies for dealing with these risks, such as reducing, avoiding, or accepting them.
3. **Compliance** :
   - Ensures that the organization adheres to laws, regulations, and standards relevant to its industry.
   - Includes complying with local and international laws regarding security, privacy, taxes, and more.
